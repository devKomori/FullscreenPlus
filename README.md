**FullscreenPlus is a browser extension that gives you control over video fullscreen behavior and playback volume.**

> I created this extension because I wanted to watch videos or shows in fullscreen on one half of my ultrawide monitor while multitasking on the other.
>
> I tried a few existing extensions, but they either didn't work on most websites, or had some UI bugs that required refreshing the page to fix.
> I also didn't see the point of installing a separate extension just to boost the volume occasionally, so I decided to include that functionality as well.
> 
> The result is an all-in-one extension designed to improve the video-watching experience. The extension works on most video platforms I've tested, but some websites may handle fullscreen differently or use custom video players, so full compatibility isn't guaranteed.

## Features

- **Native Fullscreen**: Use the browser's default fullscreen behavior.
- **In-Window Fullscreen**: Make videos fill your browser window instead of your entire screen.
- **Ask Mode**: Choose your preferred fullscreen behavior each time.
- **Volume Booster**: Boost tab audio up to 400%.
- **Per-Site Preferences**: Remembers your preferred fullscreen mode for each website.

Volume defaults to 100% when navigating to a new page or refreshing, preventing unexpectedly loud playback.

## Browser Support
- **Chrome and Edge** - Fullscreen and tab audio boosting.
- **Firefox** - Fullscreen controls and volume boosting with some website limitations.

Firefox uses a different audio capturing method compared to Chrome so due to browser API limitations, the volume boosting may not work on every website.

## Permissions & Privacy
FullscreenPlus requires permission to access and modify site content to customize fullscreen behavior and provide volume boosting. You can change this in settings to only work on sites you grant access.
